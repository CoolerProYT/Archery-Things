# FAQ

### My bow ignores the quiver and uses arrows from my inventory.

The selected slot is empty. You'll see **No arrow in selected quiver slot.** when you draw. Open the menu with <kbd>R</kbd> and select a slot with arrows in it.

### Pressing R does nothing.

<kbd>R</kbd> only works when you're **wearing** a quiver: in your chest slot, bound to your chestplate or leggings, or in a Curios/Trinkets slot. If it's just in your inventory, hold it and right-click. The key can also be changed in **Controls**, so check it hasn't been rebound or clashes with another mod.

### Can I put potions or other items in the quiver?

No. The slots only take arrows (including tipped and spectral arrows, and arrows from other mods) and firework rockets.

### Does Infinity work?

Yes. With Infinity, shooting doesn't use up arrows from the quiver.

### How do I take a quiver off my chestplate?

Drop the chestplate on the ground and drop an anvil on it. See [Binding to armor](./guide/armor#getting-the-quiver-back).

### Can I bind two quivers?

One per armor piece, so you can have one on your chestplate and one on your leggings. The chestplate's quiver is used first.

### Arrows I pick up aren't going into the quiver.

Check the button in the top-right corner of the quiver menu. It should say **Q**, not **I**. If the quiver is full, arrows go to your inventory. Arrow pickup into the quiver doesn't happen in Creative mode.
