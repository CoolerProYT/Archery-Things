# Getting started

Archery Things adds a **quiver**: a bag for your arrows that lets you pick which arrow you shoot, and that you can wear on your back or on your armor.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net/) with [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api), or [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/), for Minecraft **26.3**.
2. Download Archery Things and put the jar in your `mods` folder.

| Platform | Link |
| --- | --- |
| CurseForge | [Download from CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/archery-things) |
| Modrinth | [Download from Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/archery-things) |

3. Optional: add [Curios](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios) (NeoForge) or Trinkets (Fabric and NeoForge) for a dedicated quiver slot. See [Curios & Trinkets](./compat).

The mod is needed on both the client and the server.

## Your first quiver

**1. Craft a quiver** from seven leather and a chest.

<RecipeCard id="quiver" />

**2. Fill it.** Right-click with the quiver in your hand to open it, and put your arrows in the top row.

<QuiverMenu />

**3. Pick an arrow.** Press <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> or scroll while the menu is open. The selected slot has an orange outline. Try it on the menu above.

**4. Wear it.** Sneak and right-click, or put it in your chest slot. Now you can open it any time with <kbd>R</kbd>, and your bow fires whatever is in the selected slot.
