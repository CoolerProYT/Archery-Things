# Curios & Trinkets

With an accessory mod installed, the quiver gets its own equipment slot, so it doesn't take up your chest slot at all.

| Mod | Loader | Slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Curios](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios) | NeoForge | **Quiver** |
| Trinkets | Fabric, NeoForge | **Quiver**, in the chest group |

A quiver in the accessory slot works just like one you're wearing:

- press <kbd>R</kbd> to open the quiver menu
- your bow or crossbow uses its selected slot
- picked-up arrows go into it

The accessory slot is checked **last**, after your chest slot and any quivers bound to your armor. See [which quiver is used](./quiver#shooting).

Both mods are optional. Without them, the quiver goes in your chest slot or is [bound to armor](./armor).
